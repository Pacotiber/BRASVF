PIVOT HARDWARE OF THE OLD GEAR GRIPPER (02_Old_gripper) - McMaster-Carr references
==================================================================================

Quantity: 7 screws + 7 nuts
  - link/finger pivots x2, gear/finger x2
  - pivots on the frame: links x2, driven gear x1
  (the drive gear is carried by the SG90 shaft, no screw)

1) Shoulder screw - McMaster 91273A322
   https://www.mcmaster.com/91273A322/
   Same-Size Thread 18-8 Stainless Steel Shoulder Screw
   - Smooth shoulder: D3 mm (tol. -0.04 / -0.01), length 6 mm (tol. 0 / +0.25)
   - Thread: M3 x 0.5, length 4 mm
   - Head: D5 x 2 mm, 2 mm hex socket
   - Indicative price: $4.96 each ($4.20 from 5)
   File: CAD/Screws_McMaster/91273A322_shoulder_screw_D3_L6_M3.SLDPRT

2) Nylon-insert locknut - McMaster 93625A100
   https://www.mcmaster.com/93625A100/
   18-8 Stainless Steel Nylon-Insert Locknut, M3 x 0.5
   - 5.5 mm across flats, 4 mm high
   - Indicative price: $6.94 for a pack of 100
   File: CAD/Screws_McMaster/93625A100_nylon_locknut_M3.SLDPRT

Principle: the two plates (2 x 3 mm = 6 mm) turn on the smooth shoulder.
The nut tightens against the end of the shoulder (not against the plates), so the
pivot stays free; the nylon insert stops the nut from coming loose.

Note: these two .SLDPRT files were modelled from the dimensions of the McMaster data sheets
(downloading the official CAD requires a logged-in McMaster account).
To replace them with the official files: log in on mcmaster.com, "Download" button
(3-D SolidWorks) on each product page, then in SolidWorks right-click the component >
Replace Components.

Other McMaster parts in this folder:
  - 90265A189 18-8 stainless steel shoulder screw (used by the arm assemblies)
  - 57155K352 stainless steel ball bearing

Printed alternative to the shoulder screws:
  CAD/Screws_McMaster/Shoulder_screw_holder.SLDPRT (and Gcode/Shoulder_screw_holder.3mf) is a printed
  sleeve, D5 mm outside, D3 mm bore, 5 mm long. Slide it onto a standard M3 screw: the joint turns
  on the sleeve and the M3 screw only holds it. It does the job on this arm.
